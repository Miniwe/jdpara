# E-A01. How long it takes to draft one blog post

## Hypothesis

A draft post about the watering tracker will take no more than two
hours, writing from the already-finished [[(P) Projects/01 Example of a mature project/README|project README]].

## Method

Time it from opening a blank file to a draft that isn't embarrassing to
show someone.

## Numbers

Took 3 hours 40 minutes — almost twice the estimate, mostly from
re-explaining the sensor calibration.

## Conclusion

Hypothesis not confirmed. Factored into
[[../30 Decisions/D-A01-dont-start-a-blog-until-enough-material|D-A01]]
as an extra argument — the cost of one post is higher than it seemed.
