# ⚙️ A · Areas

**Test data.** PARA: Areas — areas of ongoing responsibility with no
fixed finish line: thinking and decision-making outside the scope of any
single project. Unlike `(P) Projects`, this isn't "the whole greenhouse
watering tracker" — it's scattered thoughts and questions not yet shaped
into a project (or that may never become one).

| Subfolder | Role in the pipeline |
|---|---|
| [[00 Inbox/README\|00 Inbox]] | raw intake: dumps, ideas, unsorted material |
| [[10 Ideas/README\|10 Ideas]] | hypotheses not yet shaped into a question or decision |
| [[20 Questions/README\|20 Questions]] | open problems and dilemmas not tied to a project |
| [[30 Decisions/README\|30 Decisions]] | a fixed choice (ADR) outside the scope of a single project |
| [[40 Experiments/README\|40 Experiments]] | R&D sandbox: hypothesis → method → conclusion |

Flow: `Inbox → Ideas → Questions → Experiments → Decisions`, and beyond
that — settled knowledge moves into `(R) Resources/15 Knowledge` rather
than staying here. The difference from `(P) Projects`: there the
pipeline is tied to a single goal with a horizon; here it's tied to an
area of responsibility with no finish line.
